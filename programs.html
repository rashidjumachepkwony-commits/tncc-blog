Programs

Initiatives built for participation, wellbeing and growth.

Every TNCC program is designed with the community, delivered with local partners, and open to everyone.

Sport

Cross-country & wellness

Inclusive running events, fitness sessions and community rallies that bring people together in a positive, supportive environment.

  • Races for every age and ability
  • Community training sessions
  • Health and fitness awareness
Youth

Youth leadership

Programs that create room for young people to participate, learn, lead and take part in community action.

  • Mentorship and coaching
  • Confidence building
  • Skills and talent development
Dialogue

Community conversation

Listening sessions and open forums where community members surface challenges and shape practical responses together.

  • Community listening sessions
  • Shared problem-solving
  • Local leader engagement
Equality

Gender-based violence awareness

We use the platform of sport to raise awareness, challenge harmful norms and stand with survivors — building safer communities for women and girls.

  • Awareness campaigns at every event
  • Community pledges and education
  • Partnerships for safer environments
How we work

Our program approach is simple and people-centered.

1. Listen

We learn from community voices and understand local realities before shaping action.

2. Act

We organize practical events, opportunities and support systems for participation and growth.

3. Measure

We evaluate engagement and the impact of each initiative to improve and expand reach.
