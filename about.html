About us

Creating safer, healthier communities through sport.

Who we are

TNCC is a community-led movement.

Teso North Cross Country CBO works at the intersection of sport, community wellbeing, youth development, and social change. We believe movement can build connection and create real opportunities for collective action.

Our focus

  • Healthy, active communities
  • Youth leadership and participation
  • Safer local environments
  • Local partnerships and accountability
Mission & vision

What guides us.

Mission

To use sport and community participation to promote wellbeing, resilience, dignity, and positive social change in Teso North.

A community where people are healthy, included, empowered, and connected through shared action.

Our story

Rooted in local action.

TNCC began as a grassroots effort to bring people together around sport, wellbeing, and community participation. Over time, the movement expanded to include youth engagement, leadership, dialogue, and deeper social impact work. The strength of the organization lies in its local roots, community trust, and commitment to long-term collaboration.

Partners

Community partnerships matter.

We work with local leaders, families, organizations, volunteers, and stakeholders who share our goal of building healthier and more united communities.
