About the movement

Teso North Cross Country

Teso North Cross Country is presented as a community sports platform where running creates opportunities for people to connect, participate and promote positive social change.

The movement's message is simple: RUN. UNITE. TRANSFORM. Sport can open conversations, strengthen relationships and encourage people to take collective action for healthier and more resilient communities.

Mission

To use inclusive sport and community dialogue to build healthier, safer and more connected communities.

Vision

A resilient society where every person has a place to participate, be heard and thrive.

Our history

TNCC began as a local running initiative in Teso North and grew through shared action by runners, families and community partners.

Our promise

We make participation welcoming, transparent and centered on dignity, equality and collective responsibility.
