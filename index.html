About the movement

Teso North Cross Country

Teso North Cross Country is presented as a community sports platform where running creates opportunities for people to connect, participate and promote positive social change.

The movement's message is simple: RUN. UNITE. TRANSFORM. Sport can open conversations, strengthen relationships and encourage people to take collective action for healthier and more resilient communities.
